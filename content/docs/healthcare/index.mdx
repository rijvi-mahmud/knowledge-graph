Every healthcare application handles protected health information, works with
payers, and needs a patient's consent before care is recorded. Those
obligations do not change between an EHR, a dental practice system or a billing
platform, so they are specified once here and inherited by every domain below.

## Domains

Each domain covers patients, appointments and the rest from its own purpose,
and adds the modules that exist only in that kind of application.

| Domain | Builds on the shared layer with | Status |
|---|---|---|
| EHR | Encounters, clinical notes | Not yet specified |
| [Dental](/docs/healthcare/dental) | Treatment plans, chair scheduling, recall | In progress |
| Mental health | Recurring sessions, therapy notes | Not yet specified |
| RCM | Claims, denials, remittance | Not yet specified |
