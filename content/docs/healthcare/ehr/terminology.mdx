Terminology in the EHR is the [healthcare terminology specification](/docs/healthcare/terminology)
as it stands. This page records that it was reviewed for hospitals and needs
nothing more.

## A hospital needs nothing more

The rules a hospital might seem to need, such as coding inpatient procedures
with ICD-10-PCS in the United States or using the discharge date for
inpatient claims, are already part of the healthcare rules, because any
healthcare application that bills inpatient stays needs them.
