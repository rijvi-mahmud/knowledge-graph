Meeting scheduling, room reservation and calendar applications need exactly what
the [core specification](/docs/core/appointment) already provides: a time range, participants, a
status lifecycle and reminders. There are no regulatory gates to satisfy and no
downstream record to create.

This layer is documented rather than omitted so that "reviewed, nothing to add"
remains distinguishable from "nobody has looked at this yet."

## Where find-a-time belongs

Searching for a slot that suits several participants is a Calendar capability,
not an Appointment one. Appointment queries availability rather than computing
it, and moving that search here would pull working hours, time zones and
resource rules into a module that deliberately excludes them.

The same applies to recurring meetings. A recurrence rule expands into
individual appointments; the expansion belongs upstream, and each resulting
appointment follows the core lifecycle unchanged.
