---
title: Electronic health record (EHR)
description: The electronic health record for hospitals and clinics, from registration to discharge.
---

An electronic health record (EHR) holds a patient's record across every visit
and stay: who they are, when they're seen, what was found, and what was done.
The modules below are specified in the order a hospital system is usually
built, because each one depends on the ones before it.

## Patients and appointments come first

Every clinical module refers to a patient, and most clinical work starts from a
visit. So the shared modules come first, and each later module builds on them.

| Order | Module | Covers | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Patients](/docs/healthcare/ehr/patient) | Registration, demographics, identifiers and duplicate records, built on the healthcare [patient record rules](/docs/healthcare/patient) | Draft |
| 2 | [Appointments](/docs/healthcare/ehr/appointment) | Scheduling for outpatient and virtual visits and for patients in a hospital stay, built on the [core Appointment specification](/docs/core/appointment) | Draft |
| 3 | [Encounters](/docs/healthcare/ehr/encounter) | Emergency visits and inpatient stays, built on the healthcare [clinical encounter rules](/docs/healthcare/encounter) | Draft |
| 4 | [Admission, discharge and transfer](/docs/healthcare/ehr/adt) | Inpatient admission, bed and ward assignment, transfers and discharge | Draft |
| 5 | [Clinical documentation](/docs/healthcare/ehr/clinical-documentation) | Hospital notes and deadlines, built on the healthcare [clinical notes and observations rules](/docs/healthcare/clinical-documentation) | Draft |
| 6 | Orders and results | Laboratory and imaging orders, and their results | Not yet specified |
| 7 | Medications and allergies | Prescribing, administration records and allergy lists, including US rules for prescribing controlled substances by telemedicine (DEA flexibilities run to 31 December 2026) | Not yet specified |
| 8 | Organization | Departments, rooms, equipment and which practitioners work where | Not yet specified |
| 9 | [Practitioners and credentialing](/docs/healthcare/ehr/practitioner) | Medical staff appointment and clinical privileges, built on the healthcare [practitioner rules](/docs/healthcare/practitioner) | Draft |
| 10 | [Identity and access](/docs/healthcare/ehr/identity) | Shared clinical workstations with badge tap and fast user switching, and downtime access, built on the healthcare [identity rules](/docs/healthcare/identity) | Draft |
| 11 | Consent | Consents and authorizations, privacy notice acknowledgment, 42 CFR Part 2 consent, GDPR consent records, EHDS restrictions and opt-outs (Articles 8 and 10), and the US Protecting Care Access exception for reproductive health information (45 CFR 171.206) | Not yet specified |
| 12 | [Terminology](/docs/healthcare/ehr/terminology) | Code systems and their releases, value sets and maps, built on the healthcare [terminology rules](/docs/healthcare/terminology) | Draft |
| 13 | Referrals | Referrals into specialist departments, and whether one is valid | Not yet specified |
| 14 | Waitlist | Patients waiting for a slot, and offering freed slots | Not yet specified |
| 15 | Authorization | Prior authorization requests to payers, and their decisions | Not yet specified |
| 16 | Theatre | Operating theatre lists and surgical case scheduling | Not yet specified |
| 17 | Emergency department | Emergency arrivals, triage and treatment | Not yet specified |
| 18 | Coverage | Insurance, eligibility checks, cost estimates and financial clearance | Not yet specified |
| 19 | Billing | Accounts, charges, good faith estimates, coding and claims | Not yet specified |
| 20 | Release of information | Copies of the record, accounting of disclosures, and the US single-patient electronic health information export | Not yet specified |
| 21 | Restraint and seclusion | Orders, monitoring and documentation when a patient is restrained or secluded | Not yet specified |

The list follows the functional areas of the HL7 EHR System Functional Model
(ISO 10781:2023): care provision, its support, administration, and the record
and trust infrastructure every module relies on.
