businessRules:
- id: BR-E1
  text: On a shared clinical workstation, a staff member's first sign-in of a session is at their role's required_aal. Within session_max_duration, a badge tap with their PIN resumes their own session on any shared workstation; the badge only identifies them and counts as a factor only if it is a cryptographic smart card. The next user's tap hides the previous user's work on that workstation, and one user's screen never carries over to another.
  rationale: Clinicians sign in many times an hour on shared workstations. NIST SP 800-63B-4 lets an AAL2 session resume with a password and the session secret after inactivity, so a tap and PIN are fast without lowering assurance, and every action stays attributed to the right person (45 CFR 164.312(a)(2)(i)).
- id: BR-E2
  text: Shared clinical workstations lock after shared_workstation_timeout of inactivity, hiding patient data; the user's session pauses after session_inactivity_timeout as on any device.
  rationale: Screens in clinical areas are seen by passers-by, so they lock sooner than a session pauses; HIPAA expects sessions to end after inactivity (45 CFR 164.312(a)(2)(iii)).
- id: BR-E3
  text: During an EHR downtime, staff reach the read-only backup with their own accounts, verified without the EHR, such as against the organization's directory, and every access is logged; the backup never accepts changes.
  rationale: The SAFER Contingency Planning guide expects a read-only backup during downtime; if sign-in needed the EHR that is down, nobody could reach it, and access must still be attributable to a person.

Hospital sign-in builds on the [healthcare identity rules](/docs/healthcare/identity).
This layer adds the shared clinical workstation, where a nurse and a doctor
may sign in to the same screen minutes apart.

## Switching users takes seconds, never a shared login

Each person signs in fully once per session. After that, a badge tap and a PIN
bring their session back on any shared workstation and hide the last
person's work, so nobody is tempted to keep working under someone else's
name (BR-E1).
