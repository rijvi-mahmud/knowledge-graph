title: Patients
description: 'Hospital patient registration: medical record numbers, unidentified emergency patients, newborns, identification bands, quick registration and privacy aliases.'
module: patient

industry: healthcare

purpose: 'Add what a hospital needs to identify patients: a medical record number for everyone, patients who arrive unconscious or unknown, babies born in the hospital, identification bands, registration that never delays emergency care, and aliases for patients whose identity needs protecting.'
scope:
- Assigning the hospital medical record number (MRN)
- Registering unidentified patients under an alias, and identifying them later
- Registering newborns with distinct names, linked to their mother
- The data printed on identification bands
- Quick registration with minimal data, completed later
- Registering a patient under a privacy alias
- Registering patients while the registration system is down, and reconciling them after
- Stillborn babies, where the hospital records them as patients
nonGoals:
- Emergency department arrival, triage and treatment. They belong to the Emergency department module, which registers patients through this one.
- Admissions and bed assignment. They belong to the Admission, discharge and transfer module.
- Printing bands and labels. A printing service prints what this module returns.
- Birth registration with civil authorities. This module records the newborn as a patient only.
actors:
- name: Emergency department staff
  description: Register patients who arrive in an emergency, including unidentified ones.
- name: Maternity staff
  description: Register babies born in the hospital.
assumptions:
- id: AS-E1
  text: The hospital has one MRN system for each scope set in mrn_scope, and this module is the only one that assigns numbers in it.
  rationale: Two systems assigning MRNs from the same range would give two patients one number.
glossary:
- term: Medical record number (MRN)
  definition: The number a hospital assigns to identify a patient's record. One of the identifiers The Joint Commission lists for patient identification.
- term: Unidentified patient
  definition: A patient whose identity is unknown on arrival, such as an unconscious trauma patient, registered under an alias.
- term: Alias
  definition: A temporary name given to an unidentified patient, from the hospital's alias scheme, such as a phonetic word and a number.
- term: Identification band
  definition: The wristband a patient wears in hospital, showing identifiers and a barcode.
- term: Quick registration
  definition: Registering with the minimum data so care can start, and completing the details later.

businessRules:
- id: BR-H1
  text: Before a new patient is registered, existing records are searched with at least the name and birth date. If the search finds a probable match, the registrar either picks that record or registers a new one with a reason. Unidentified patients and babies born in the hospital are registered without a search.
  rationale: HL7 FHIR notes that around 2% of registrations are in error, mostly duplicate records. An unidentified patient has nothing to search with, and a baby born here cannot have a record yet. Both are checked later (BR-E3).
- id: BR-H4
  text: A record has at least one usual or official name, a birth date and an administrative gender. The birth date can be partial or estimated, and the gender can be unknown, when that is all anyone knows. An unidentified patient has a temp alias as their only name and an estimated birth year.
  rationale: Name and birth date are what every search and identity check rely on. An unidentified patient still needs both to be told apart, so the alias and an estimate stand in for them.
- id: BR-E1
  text: Every patient gets an MRN from mrn_system at registration, including its check digit. An MRN is never reused, including after a merge, where it stays on the target marked old.
  rationale: The Joint Commission lists an assigned identification number, such as the MRN, as a patient identifier (NPG.01.01.01), and the ONC SAFER Guide recommends a check digit to catch typing errors. Reusing an MRN would make old documents and labels point at the wrong person.
- id: BR-E2
  text: An unidentified patient is registered with an alias from alias_scheme and marked unidentified. An alias is never given to two records, is recognisable as temporary, and differs in look and sound from every other alias in use, including when shortened on screens and labels.
  rationale: Placeholder names such as John Doe cause duplicates and mix-ups. The SAFER Guide asks for temporary ids that are recognisable as temporary, distinguishable by look and sound in full and shortened forms, and not dehumanising. A UK trauma centre found staff confused similar-sounding aliases.
- id: BR-E3
  text: When an unidentified patient is identified, the hospital searches for an existing record. If one is found, the alias record is merged into it. If not, the real details are added, the alias is kept as an old name, and unidentified is cleared. Either way patient.identified is emitted.
  rationale: Care given under the alias must stay with the person. The same study found record merging after identification was done inconsistently, so it is one defined step.
- id: BR-E4
  text: A baby born in the hospital is registered with a distinct name from newborn_naming, such as "Smith, Judy Girl", with a letter for each baby in a multiple birth, its birth order, born_at, and mother_patient_id. When the baby's name is given, it becomes official and the newborn name is kept as old.
  rationale: The Joint Commission requires distinct identification methods for newborns, giving "Smith, Judy Girl A" and "Smith, Judy Girl B" as examples (NPG.01.01.01 EP 3). Babies cannot say who they are and often share names such as Baby Boy Smith.
- id: BR-E5
  text: An identification band shows at least two person-specific identifiers, such as name, birth date and MRN, and a barcode that encodes them. It never shows a room or bed.
  rationale: The Joint Commission requires at least two identifiers and never the room number, and lists barcoding that includes two or more identifiers (NPG.01.01.01 EP 1). The band is what staff check before each test and treatment.
- id: BR-E6
  text: A patient who needs care at once can be registered with only what BR-H4 requires, as registration_status incomplete. Incomplete registrations are listed for staff to complete. Insurance and payment questions are never asked before the patient is seen.
  rationale: In the United States, a hospital may not delay a medical screening examination or treatment to ask about payment or insurance, and registration must not discourage patients from staying (42 CFR 489.24(d)(4)).
- id: BR-E7
  text: A patient can be registered under a privacy alias, recorded as a name with use anonymous, with confidentiality restricted or very restricted. Lists, boards and bands show the alias; the official name is shown only when the record is opened with a reason.
  rationale: FHIR defines the anonymous name use to protect a person's identity for privacy reasons. Public figures and people at risk of harm are found through ward boards and lists, not only through records.
- id: BR-E8
  text: While the registration system is unavailable, patients get ids from a pre-allocated downtime_id_pool. When it is back, each downtime registration is entered, searched for, and merged into an existing record or kept as a new one; until then it is listed for follow-up.
  rationale: The SAFER Guide asks for unique temporary ids when registration is unavailable or overwhelmed, later merged into permanent ones, with their resolution monitored.
- id: BR-E9
  text: Matching never treats an alias or a newborn name as evidence that two records are the same person; it compares the other details.
  rationale: Aliases and newborn names follow a pattern, so two unrelated records can look alike by name alone.
- id: BR-E10
  text: A patient who dies before being identified keeps the alias record, with the death recorded on it. They can still be identified later, which merges or renames the record as usual.
  rationale: Identification after death matters to the family and the medical examiner, and the care given must stay with the person.
- id: BR-E11
  text: Whether a stillborn baby is registered as a patient is set by register_stillbirths. When registered, the baby gets a newborn name and mother link, with the death recorded at birth.
  rationale: 'Practice varies, and fetal death reporting differs by state: most report from 20 weeks of gestation or 350 grams, a few at any gestation (CDC). A deployment chooses, so the record and any care given to the mother stay consistent.'
- id: BR-E12
  text: When a temporary name or id is replaced, by identification, naming, reconciliation or merge, every system using it is told, and bands and labels are reprinted.
  rationale: The SAFER Guide asks that any downstream use of a temporary id, including at transfer facilities, is tracked and corrected in every system.
- id: BR-E13
  text: Where mrn_scope is facility, each hospital's MRN is an identifier on the one patient record. Registering at a sister hospital searches the whole organisation and adds that hospital's MRN to the existing record instead of creating another. Records found for the same person at two hospitals are merged like duplicates.
  rationale: The ONC SAFER Guide calls more than one id for the same person across facilities an overlap, usually after institutions merge, and expects an enterprise index that holds every MRN with one primary key.

workflows:
- name: Register an unidentified patient
  trigger: A patient arrives who cannot say who they are and has no identification
  actor: Emergency department staff
  steps:
  - Take the next unused alias from alias_scheme
  - Record the alias, an estimated birth year and the administrative gender if known, as unidentified
  - Assign an MRN and print the band
  - Emit patient.registered
  outcome: Care starts at once, under a name nobody else has.
- name: Identify a patient
  trigger: Staff learn who an unidentified patient is
  actor: Registrar
  steps:
  - Search with the real name and birth date
  - If a record exists, merge the alias record into it
  - Otherwise add the real details, keep the alias as an old name, and clear unidentified
  - Emit patient.identified, and reprint the band
  outcome: The patient's care since arrival is on their one record.
- name: Register a newborn
  trigger: A baby is born in the hospital
  actor: Maternity staff
  steps:
  - Build the name from newborn_naming, with a letter for each baby in a multiple birth
  - Record born_at, birth order, administrative gender and mother_patient_id
  - Assign an MRN and print the band
  - Emit patient.registered
  outcome: Each baby has a distinct name and band from birth.
- name: Complete a quick registration
  trigger: A patient registered as incomplete can now give their details
  actor: Registrar
  steps:
  - Search for an existing record with the details given
  - If one exists, merge the quick record into it
  - Otherwise add the missing details and set registration_status complete
  outcome: No quick registration is left as a duplicate.
- name: Reconcile downtime registrations
  trigger: Registration comes back after an outage
  actor: Registrar
  steps:
  - Enter each downtime registration with its downtime id
  - Search for an existing record
  - Merge into it if found, otherwise keep the new record
  - Tell every system that used the downtime id, and reprint bands
  outcome: No downtime id is left unresolved.

settings:
- name: mrn_system
  type: identifier system
  description: The system MRNs are assigned in, their format and check digit scheme. MRNs carry the HL7 v2 Table 0203 type MR. Required.
- name: mrn_scope
  type: enterprise | facility
  description: Whether one MRN covers every hospital in the organisation or each hospital assigns its own. Practice varies, so each organisation chooses. Required.
- name: alias_scheme
  type: pattern
  description: How aliases for unidentified patients are made, such as a phonetic alphabet word and a number. Aliases must be recognisable as temporary, distinct by look and sound even when shortened, and not offensive. Required.
- name: newborn_naming
  type: pattern
  description: How newborn names are built, such as the mother's family and given name, the baby's administrative gender, and a letter for multiples. Required.
- name: downtime_id_pool
  type: id range
  description: Temporary ids set aside for registration outages, distributed to departments in advance. Required.
- name: register_stillbirths
  type: boolean
  description: Whether stillborn babies are registered as patients (BR-E11). Practice varies, so there is no default. Required where the hospital delivers babies.

operations:
- name: Search patients
  actor: Registrar
  description: Find patients by name, birth date, identifier, phone or address, including old values, or list incomplete and unidentified registrations.
  method: GET
  path: /patients
  success: 200
  input: any of name, birth date, identifier, phone, address, registration status, unidentified; limit and cursor
  output: matching records with enough detail to tell them apart, masked where BR-H23 applies, and aliases in place of names where BR-E7 applies
  errors:
  - PATIENT_INVALID_QUERY
  - PATIENT_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE
- name: Register an unidentified patient
  actor: Emergency department staff
  description: Register a patient whose identity is unknown, under a new alias. Accepts a retry key.
  method: POST
  path: /patients/unidentified
  success: 201
  input: estimated birth year, administrative gender if known, optional description
  output: the record with its alias and MRN
  errors:
  - PATIENT_INVALID_BIRTH_DATE
  - PATIENT_INVALID_CODE
  - PATIENT_IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED
  - PATIENT_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE
- name: Identify a patient
  actor: Registrar
  description: Record who an unidentified patient is, merging into an existing record if there is one.
  method: POST
  path: /patients/{id}/identify
  success: 200
  input: patient id, revision, the real details or the existing patient id
  output: the record in use
  errors:
  - PATIENT_NOT_FOUND
  - PATIENT_NOT_UNIDENTIFIED
  - PATIENT_REVISION_CONFLICT
  - PATIENT_INVALID_NAME
  - PATIENT_INVALID_BIRTH_DATE
  - PATIENT_DUPLICATE_IDENTIFIER
  - PATIENT_MERGED
  - PATIENT_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE
- name: Register a newborn
  actor: Maternity staff
  description: Register a baby born in the hospital. Accepts a retry key.
  method: POST
  path: /patients/newborns
  success: 201
  input: mother patient id, born_at, administrative gender, birth order for multiples, and whether stillborn where register_stillbirths is on
  output: the record with its newborn name and MRN
  errors:
  - PATIENT_INVALID_MOTHER
  - PATIENT_INVALID_BIRTH_DATE
  - PATIENT_INVALID_CODE
  - PATIENT_IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED
  - PATIENT_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE
- name: Get identification band data
  actor: Registrar
  description: What to print on the patient's band.
  method: GET
  path: /patients/{id}/identification-band
  success: 200
  input: patient id
  output: the name or alias to show, birth date, MRN and barcode content
  errors:
  - PATIENT_NOT_FOUND
  - PATIENT_MERGED
  - PATIENT_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE
- name: Enter a downtime registration
  actor: Registrar
  description: Enter a patient registered on paper during an outage, with its downtime id.
  method: POST
  path: /patients/downtime
  success: 201
  input: downtime id, and the details recorded during the outage
  output: the record, with any probable match for reconciliation
  errors:
  - PATIENT_INVALID_DOWNTIME_ID
  - PATIENT_INVALID_NAME
  - PATIENT_INVALID_BIRTH_DATE
  - PATIENT_INVALID_CODE
  - PATIENT_IDEMPOTENCY_KEY_REUSED
  - PATIENT_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE
- name: Report temporary ids
  actor: Health information management (HIM) specialist
  description: Unresolved aliases, downtime ids and incomplete registrations, oldest first.
  method: GET
  path: /patients/temporary-ids
  success: 200
  input: optional kind and minimum age; limit and cursor
  output: records with their kind and age
  errors:
  - PATIENT_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE

Patients build on the [patient record rules](/docs/healthcare/patient)
every healthcare application shares. This layer adds what happens at a
hospital's front door, in its emergency department and on its maternity ward.

## A patient nobody can name still gets a unique one

An unconscious patient gets an alias nobody else has had (BR-E2), so care can
start at once without mixing them up with the patient in the next bay. Once
someone knows who they are, the alias record joins their real one (BR-E3).

## Babies are told apart from birth

Newborns can't say who they are and often share a surname. Each one gets a
distinct name built from the mother's name, with a letter for twins and
triplets, and a link to the mother (BR-E4).

## Registration never stands between a patient and care

A patient who needs care now is registered with the minimum, and the rest is
completed later (BR-E6). Nothing about payment is asked first.

## The band is what staff check

Every test and treatment starts by checking the band against two identifiers,
so the band carries two of them and a barcode, and never the bed (BR-E5).
