variants:
- id: emergency
  title: Emergency encounters
  description: Care in the emergency department, from arrival until the patient leaves, is admitted or is transferred.
  summary: 'An emergency encounter (HL7 class EMER) is opened when the Emergency department module registers an arrival. Its subject status follows the patient: arrived, triaged, receiving care, departed. A patient who leaves before being seen is recorded, not deleted.'
- id: inpatient
  title: Inpatient encounters
  description: A hospital stay, admitted or under observation, from admission to discharge.
  summary: An inpatient encounter (HL7 class IMP, or OBSENC under observation) is opened when the Admission, discharge and transfer module admits the patient. It records the admission, every location and attending practitioner during the stay, and the discharge, and stays open until the record is complete.
purpose: 'Add what a hospital needs to the clinical encounter rules: emergency visits and inpatient stays as Admission, discharge and transfer and the Emergency department module report them, class changes such as observation to inpatient, one attending at a time, pre-admission, the US observation notice, and the facility directory.'
scope:
- 'Emergency arrivals and inpatient stays: opening, triage status, admission, transfers, leave and discharge as Admission, discharge and transfer and the Emergency department module report them'
- Class changes such as observation to inpatient, with one attending at a time
- 'Pre-admission: planned stays before the patient arrives'
- The start of observation, for the US notice deadline
- One open stay per patient, and the facility directory
nonGoals:
- Admission orders, beds, transfers and discharge orders. The Admission, discharge and transfer module owns them and reports them here.
- Triage and emergency department flow. The Emergency department module owns them and reports arrivals and triage here.

- name: System
  description: Another module, such as Appointments or Admission, discharge and transfer, reporting an event.
assumptions:
- id: AS-E4
  text: In the hospital, the Emergency department module reports arrivals and triage, and Admission, discharge and transfer reports admissions, transfers, leave and discharges, as they happen.
  rationale: Those modules own the hospital's patient flow; an encounter records what they report.
glossary:
- term: Attending
  definition: The practitioner responsible for the patient's care during the encounter (HL7 participation ATND).

  type: admission
  required: false

  constraints: 'codes: re-admission from HL7 v2 Table 0092 (R); admit source from HL7 admit-source (such as emd, outp, hosp-trans, born)'
  variant: inpatient
- name: directory_status
  type: enum
  required: false
  description: Whether the patient is listed in the facility directory during the stay.
  constraints: 'not_asked | listed | opted_out; codes: none standard, local to this specification'
  variant: inpatient
- name: status
  type: enum
  required: true
  description: Where the encounter is in its lifecycle.
  constraints: 'planned | in_progress | on_hold | discharged | completed | cancelled | discontinued | entered_in_error; changed only through operations; codes: FHIR R5 EncounterStatus (in-progress, on-hold, entered-in-error)'
- name: locations
  type: location stay[]
  required: false
  description: Locations the patient has been in, each with an entry id, period and status, owned by the Organization module. The id lets a past movement be corrected.
  constraints: 'status planned | active | reserved | completed (FHIR): planned for a pending transfer, reserved for a bed held while the patient is elsewhere; at most one active'
businessRules:
- id: BR-E3
  text: An emergency encounter is opened, in progress with subject status arrived, when the Emergency department module registers an arrival. Its subject status follows triage, care and departure as that module reports them.
  rationale: In an emergency department the patient's position changes fast and matters for safety and for billing; FHIR tracks it as subject status.
  variant: emergency
- id: BR-E4
  text: An inpatient or observation encounter is opened, in progress, when Admission, discharge and transfer reports an admission. Transfers add locations and close the previous one; leave puts the encounter on hold with subject status on leave; discharge sets discharged with the disposition and destination.
  rationale: The admission, transfers and discharge happen in that module; the encounter records them so the stay is one record from admission to discharge (FHIR admission component).
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E5
  text: A change of class, such as observation to inpatient, keeps the same encounter and adds the new class to class_history with the time it took effect.
  rationale: Medicare counts observation as outpatient care until an inpatient order, so the time in each class matters to billing; FHIR keeps class history (EncounterHistory).
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E11
  text: An inpatient or observation encounter has exactly one attending practitioner at any time. Every participant holds a role in the department on the date (Organization).
  rationale: Someone must be responsible for an admitted patient at every moment. A practitioner without a role there cannot be accountable for care in it.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E12
  text: An encounter has at most one active location at a time, and each location's period is within the encounter's. When the patient goes somewhere temporarily, such as the scanner or dialysis, their bed becomes reserved and the temporary location active; on return the temporary location completes and the bed is active again.
  rationale: The patient is in one place, but their bed is still theirs. FHIR marks a held location reserved, and IHE tracks temporary transfers to radiotherapy, scanners and dialysis as their own movements.
- id: BR-E21
  text: A patient has at most one inpatient or observation encounter that is in progress or on hold. A patient merge can break this; BR-E29 handles it.
  rationale: A patient is in one hospital stay at a time. Two open stays send orders, results and charges to the wrong one.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E23
  text: 'When an encounter enters observation, encounter.observation_started is emitted with observation_notice_due_at: the start plus observation_notice_deadline. Deciding who must receive the notice, and giving it, belongs to Admission, discharge and transfer.'
  rationale: US hospitals must give Medicare patients who receive observation services as outpatients for more than 24 hours a standardized written notice no later than 36 hours after observation starts, or sooner on transfer, discharge or admission (42 CFR 489.20(y)). The encounter knows when observation began.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E24
  text: A pre-admission from Admission, discharge and transfer opens a planned inpatient encounter with planned_start and the pre-admission id. The admission starts that same encounter; a cancelled pre-admission cancels it with the reason admission_cancelled.
  rationale: FHIR uses planned encounters for pre-admission, and HL7 v2 sends pre-admit (A05) and cancel pre-admit (A38). Testing and paperwork before an elective stay need an encounter to attach to.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E27
  text: A re-admission is marked on the admission when Admission, discharge and transfer reports it.
  rationale: Readmissions are tracked for quality and payment. FHIR records it as admission.reAdmission.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E29
  text: After a patient merge, if the remaining patient has two inpatient or observation encounters in progress or on hold, both are kept and encounter.patient_review_needed is emitted for each, so an HIM specialist resolves them. Merging them (BR-H48) resolves it.
  rationale: Duplicate records can each hold an open stay for the same admission. Refusing the merge keeps the duplicate; keeping both silently breaks the one-stay rule (BR-E21).
- id: BR-E36
  text: 'Pending events from Admission, discharge and transfer are recorded as plans: a pending transfer adds a planned location, a pending discharge sets planned_end, and each is removed when cancelled or replaced by the real event.'
  rationale: IHE Patient Administration Management defines pending admit, transfer and discharge (A14, A15, A16, cancelled by A27, A26, A25). Bed management and discharge planning work from who is expected to move.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E37
  text: A transfer recorded by mistake can be cancelled, which completes nothing and makes the previous location active again. An earlier movement is corrected by its entry id; on a locked encounter the correction is an amendment.
  rationale: IHE defines cancel transfer (A12) and identifies each movement so a past one can be corrected (Historic Movement option). Location-based billing and the record of where the patient was depend on it.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E38
  variant: inpatient
  text: A patient in a stay is listed in the facility directory (name and location, for people who ask by name) only after being told and not objecting, which sets directory_status listed. In an emergency or incapacity they can be listed if it fits their known wishes and best interest, and are asked as soon as they can be. An objection removes the listing at once, and restricted or very restricted patients are never listed.
  rationale: HIPAA lets a hospital keep a directory of name and location for people who ask by name, but only after telling the patient and giving them the chance to object, with an exception for incapacity or emergency until they can be asked (45 CFR 164.510(a)). Telling a caller someone is in hospital, against their wishes, can put them at risk.
- id: BR-E43
  text: When Admission, discharge and transfer changes the attending practitioner, the previous attending participation ends and the new one starts at the effective time; a cancelled change ends the new participation and restores the previous one. A cancelled leave resumes the encounter and removes the time on hold; a cancelled return puts it back on hold. A bed swap is recorded as a transfer for each encounter.
  rationale: IHE PAM sends change attending doctor (A54, cancelled by A55), cancel leave and cancel return (A52, A53) and swap patients (A17). BR-E11 needs exactly one attending at a time, and length (BR-H16) excludes leave, so both must follow the cancels.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E45
  text: 'When Admission, discharge and transfer discharges a patient who is on leave, the encounter is discharged from on hold: the leave ends at the discharge time and the disposition and destination are recorded.'
  rationale: A patient can die or decide not to return while on leave. Requiring the encounter to resume first would record care that never happened, and would leave length counting the leave (BR-H16).
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-E46
  text: When Admission, discharge and transfer cancels an admission recorded in error, the encounter it started is marked entered in error with the reason admission_cancelled. When it clears an expected discharge, planned_end is cleared.
  rationale: FHIR uses cancelled only for encounters that never started, so a started encounter opened by mistake is entered in error. ADT cancels an admission only when no clinical data is recorded, which Encounters also requires. IHE PAM cancels a pending discharge with A25.
  variant: inpatient
- id: BR-H47
  text: An encounter recorded on the wrong patient is moved to the right patient by an HIM specialist, with a reason. It keeps its id and visit number; on a locked encounter the move is also recorded as an amendment. The move is refused if the right patient is not in use or would then have two inpatient or observation encounters open (BR-E21). An encounter that belongs to a stay moves only with its stay, through Admission, discharge and transfer.
  rationale: A visit with notes on the wrong person is a record overlay, which ONC SAFER Guide 6 counts among the most dangerous identification errors. Entered in error is refused once clinical data exists, so a move is the only fix. HL7 moves a visit between patients (A45). Clinical modules keep their entries on the encounter id, so they follow it.
- id: BR-H16
  text: length is the time from actual_start to actual_end less time on leave.
  rationale: FHIR defines length as the time the encounter lasted, less time absent. Leave counts toward neither care nor billing time.

workflows:
- name: Follow an emergency visit
  trigger: The Emergency department module reports arrival, triage and departure
  actor: System
  variant: emergency
  steps:
  - Open the encounter in progress with subject status arrived
  - Set subject status triaged, then receiving care, as reported
  - On departure, discharge it, or discontinue it with the departure reason if care was not complete
  - If the patient is admitted, ADT opens an inpatient encounter; the emergency encounter is discharged
  outcome: Every emergency visit is one encounter, including patients who leave.
- name: Pre-admit a stay
  trigger: Admission, discharge and transfer reports a pre-admission
  actor: System
  variant: inpatient
  steps:
  - Open a planned inpatient encounter with planned_start and the pre-admission id
  - On admission, start this encounter instead of opening another
  - On a cancelled pre-admission, cancel it
  outcome: Pre-admission testing and paperwork attach to the stay before it begins.
- name: Follow a stay
  trigger: Admission, discharge and transfer reports an admission, transfer, leave or discharge
  actor: System
  variant: inpatient
  steps:
  - On admission, open the encounter in progress with the admission details and first location
  - On transfer, complete the previous location and add the new one
  - On leave, put the encounter on hold; on return, resume it
  - On a class change, add it to class_history
  - On discharge, set discharged with disposition and destination, and record_due_at
  outcome: The stay is one record from admission to discharge.

Hospital encounters build on the [clinical encounter rules](/docs/healthcare/encounter)
every health application shares. This layer adds emergency visits and
inpatient stays, whose movements come from the Emergency department module
and Admission, discharge and transfer.

## A stay is one record, whatever it becomes

A patient under observation who is admitted keeps the same encounter, with the
time of each class recorded (BR-E5). Transfers, leave and discharge come from
Admission, discharge and transfer and are recorded as they happen (BR-E4).

## In the United States, a hospital record is due 30 days after discharge

US hospitals must complete the record, with the final diagnosis, within 30
days of discharge, so record_completion_deadline is 30 days or less here.
