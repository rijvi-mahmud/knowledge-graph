Scheduling a financial advice session is itself a regulated act. Before a slot
can be held, the client must be verified and the advisor must be licensed for
the subject to be discussed. Both are prerequisites rather than follow-ups.

The slot behaves exactly as specified in the [core lifecycle](/docs/core/appointment). Everything
below constrains what may be booked in the first place.

## Verification blocks the booking

A booking request is rejected outright when the client's verification has not
passed. The check runs before any slot is held, and it fails closed: if the KYC
service is unreachable, the request is refused rather than allowed through for
later reconciliation.

The asymmetry justifies the strictness. A scheduled advice session with an
unverified client is a reportable breach and cannot be corrected after the
fact, whereas a booking that has to be retried is an inconvenience.

Verification is also a point-in-time assertion, not a permanent property.
Records older than the configured validity period are treated as expired and
block booking until the client re-verifies, because stale verification carries
the same regulatory exposure as none at all.

## Advice must be attributable

Every appointment names one licensed advisor, and that advisor's licence must
cover the topic being booked. Both are required at booking time rather than
resolved later.

Regulated advice has to be attributable to a named individual from the moment
it is scheduled. Advice given outside licensed scope is a personal liability for
the advisor as well as an exposure for the firm, so the licence check belongs at
the point the commitment is made.

## Overrides are a recorded path, not a workaround

Compliance officers can book past the verification gate, supplying a reason that
is written to the audit log along with their identity.

Legitimate exceptions arise regardless of what the system permits. The choice is
between an explicit path that leaves a record and an informal one that leaves
nothing behind, so the override is modelled as a first-class operation rather
than an undocumented escape hatch.

## Cancellation reasons are constrained

Reasons come from a fixed regulated list rather than free text. Cancellation
patterns are reported to regulators, and free-form reasons cannot be aggregated
into the categories those reports require.
