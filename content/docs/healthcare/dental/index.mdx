---
title: Dental
description: Practice management for general and specialist dental practices.
---

A dental practice schedules chairs as well as people, bills by procedure code,
and brings most patients back on a fixed recall cycle. Everything shared across
healthcare (consent, payer eligibility, PHI handling) applies here unchanged.

## Modules

| Module | What dental adds | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [Appointments](/docs/healthcare/dental/appointment) | Operatories, procedure-based timing, checkout posting | Draft |
| Patients | Guarantor and family accounts, medical history review | Not yet specified |
| Treatment plans | Phased treatment, acceptance, financial estimates | Not yet specified |
| Recall | Intervals per patient, recall lists | Not yet specified |
